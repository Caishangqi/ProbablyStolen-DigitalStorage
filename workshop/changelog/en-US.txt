This update keeps storage format v2. Back up your save before upgrading.

[list]
[*]Added a 3x3 Cell Workbench that needs no network or power. Partition configuration moves to the workbench; terminals handle storage and search.
[*]Partitions support whitelists, blacklists, and conditions for item, type, legality, stolen status, heat, and visible features. Empty lists accept everything.
[*]Drag real items into the rule grid without moving or consuming them. Rules support moving, copying, renaming, and undoing deletion. Valid edits save automatically.
[*]Added Type Limit and Equal Distribution modules, installed and removed at the workbench. Type limit modules have three qualities and random bonuses, with merchant prices based on quality and values.
[*]Type overhead is spread across the effective type limit without increasing byte capacity. Rule slots follow that limit; lowering it keeps existing rules.
[*]Cells accept native Module Bay Expansion Kits up to their tier's expansion limit. Take out stored items, remove modules, and delete rules before removing a cell component.
[*]Terminals and partition editors can be brought in front of native windows and vice versa.
[/list]
