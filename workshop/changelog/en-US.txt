0.7.7

Storage and transfer logs now offer clearer controls and more useful diagnostics.

- Choose a log level for each category in mod settings. Terminal traces use the interface log, while item identity details use the storage log and its identity setting.
- When an IO Port session ends without moving anything, its log explains why, lists the last refusal for each input slot, and shows the connected storage cells. The same reason is logged once until an item moves.
- When a cell is reread, unchanged items keep their known types. New or changed items are identified again.
- Item identity now ignores volatile features and retains foreign item values. Fallback handling also reports why it was used.
