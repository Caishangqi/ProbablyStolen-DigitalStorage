0.6.5

The terminal now highlights items customers want and lets you adjust quantities while dragging items out.

- Customer demand highlights mark ordinary wanted items in green, stolen items in amber, and contraband in red. During inspections, stolen items and contraband are highlighted; items in both categories use red.
- A single-slot drag starts with one item. Hold Shift as you start to take all, scroll during the drag to adjust by one, or right-click to halve the amount.
