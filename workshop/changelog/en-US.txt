0.5.12

You can now drag bottled water out of the terminal and onto the top row of the counter. This update also adds a Keep Open toggle and makes long tooltips easier to read.

- When you drag out one item, the green square marks its top-left cell. Where you grabbed the icon doesn't change the landing spot.
- When you drag out several items, the whole target inventory lights up and the game finds space for them. Reduce the quantity to one to see the single-cell preview again.
- Stored liquids stop cooling or reacting to temperature until you take them out. A bottle of water will no longer fail to come out because its temperature changed in storage.
- Turn on Keep Open in the terminal toolbar to leave the terminal open when you press Esc. The close button still works, and the setting is saved.
- Mod button tooltips fit their text. Hover over an option in the stacking attribute picker to read the full description.
